## Q1. What is the warranty?

24 months for individuals and 12 months for legal entities. It covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover mechanical damage, liquid damage, or unauthorized repairs.

## Q2. How do I make a warranty claim?

Through the form on our website or by replying to this email, including the invoice number and a photo. Our service department will respond within 2 business days. We cover the transportation costs.

## Q3. How long does a repair take?

Up to 30 calendar days. If a spare part is unavailable, we offer a replacement device or a refund.

## Q4. Can I return an order?

Yes, within 14 days of receiving it, provided the packaging is preserved. The refund is issued within 14 days.

## Q5. What are the delivery times?

Sofia – within 24 hours for orders placed before 4:00 PM on a business day. Nationwide – 1 to 3 business days. Free delivery for orders over BGN 150.

## Q6. What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery, bank transfer, and card payment. For companies – deferred payment of up to 14 days, subject to an approved credit limit.

## Q7. When will I receive my invoice?

The invoice is issued automatically after payment. Corrections can be made within 5 business days.

## Q8. What are the support hours?

Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM. Support is not available on weekends.

## Q9. How can I check the status of my order?

Using the order number in your account or by replying to the order confirmation email. We respond within 1 business day.

## Q10. How can I have my personal data deleted?

Under GDPR, you can request deletion by contacting our personal data email address. We process such requests within 30 days.
